These ten fiscal years are a separate legal entity's spending, never summed into the NHS England chart above - the two are shown side by side only for scale, not as parts of one total.     Detailed payments

    Calendar year 2024

    All twelve monthly workbooks (archive) are combined into 51,971 current rows totalling £32.327bn across NHS England and four commissioning support units. The superseded “July” workbook was recovered from the UK Government Web Archive: it repeated 3,879 June-dated rows apart from VAT identifiers. The corrected file replaces them with 4,508 July-dated rows; both sides are documented in a machine-readable correction diff.

    Historical backfill: fiscal year 2013/14 through calendar year 2023

    Twelve years of detailed payments, registered and validated against the D6 schema census: 809,371 rows totalling £311.631bn across 2013/14-2024. 2014 is April-December only - January-March 2014 are not published anywhere in the discovered corpus and are recorded as missing, not zero. Six genuine source-data shapes and defects (two stray trailing-column cases, a missing Transaction number column, a missing VAT registration number column on the largest single workbook, five rows corrupted by a pasted document URL, a non-integral invoice number and 138 rows publishing a literal zero in a text field) are each explicitly registered and published rather than silently dropped or guessed at; see "Registered workbook exceptions" in the data specification. Each year's Parquet links below; CSV.gz, Data Package, validation and checksums follow the same data/<year>-payments-over-25000/ path.

    Procurement context fixtures

    A bounded enrichment release preserves twelve Find a Tender and Contracts Finder notices across the FDP lifecycle, the Transition products framework and the Cass contract modification. Its planning estimates, tender ceilings, award and contract values, framework maxima, modification values and non-committed forecast are explicitly not payments. Five Palantir, Novartis and Pfizer supplier overlaps remain review-only name candidates and do not attribute any payment to a contract.

    Do not compare unlike totals

    The £207.963bn provider-inclusive NHS revenue limit adds trusts and foundation trusts. DHSC's £210.676bn Parliamentary resource DEL covers a still wider departmental boundary. Both are authoritative, but neither is the comparable budget for NHS England's £202.088bn audited group outturn.
