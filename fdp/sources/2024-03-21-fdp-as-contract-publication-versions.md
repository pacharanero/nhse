# FDP-AS contract publication versions

The initial notice was published on 22 December 2023 and replaced on 21 March 2024. Each notice's public versions endpoint records one version; the replacement record identifies the initial notice as its parent.

The replacement totals 532 pages and contains one text-empty page. It restores Schedule 4 as a publication part, exposes Schedule 11's personal-data processing framework, and labels remaining redactions under FOIA sections 40 and 43. It is not uniformly less redacted: some wording visible in the initial agreement, including clause 25.3, is redacted in the replacement.

The initial agreement displays 21 November 2023 as its date; the replacement displays 22 November. Contracts Finder, Find a Tender and NHS England's board update consistently record 22 November as the award, signing or start date.

No third publication generation or later formal modification notice was found in the two notice records, their APIs, the Find a Tender OCDS release chain, or targeted archive searches as of 16 October 2026. This does not prove that no operational change-control decision or unpublished contractual adjustment exists.
