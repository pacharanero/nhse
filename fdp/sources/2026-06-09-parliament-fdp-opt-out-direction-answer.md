Question tabled by Ben Obese-Jecty MP on 2 June 2026:

> To ask the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care, whether patients are able to exercise the National Data Opt-Out from inclusion in the Federated Data Platform.

Answer by Preet Kaur Gill MP, Department of Health and Social Care, on 9 June 2026:

> The National Data Opt-Out does not currently apply to the NHS Federated Data Platform (NHS FDP). This is not because of a direction but because there are currently no products within the NHS FDP which are required to apply the National Data Opt-Out. If the purpose of processing means that the National Data Opt-Out should apply, then the data controller must apply the opt-out. This is covered in the NHS FDP Information Governance Framework.

The answer linked the NHS England FDP Information Governance Framework and NHS guidance on circumstances where the National Data Opt-Out does not apply.
